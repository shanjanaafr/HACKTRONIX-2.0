Camera Calibration

Calibration finds your webcam's focal length so distance readings are accurate in real meters. Stand exactly 1 meter away from the camera, face the lens directly, then click "Capture & Calibrate".

Click "Start Camera" below

Live detected face width

-- px

  1. Start the camera above.
  2. Stand exactly 1 meter from the laptop, facing it directly.
  3. Wait until a face box appears steadily.
  4. Click Capture & Calibrate.

Formula used: f = (w_px × 1m) / 0.15m
The resulting focal length is saved to config.json and reused every time the app starts.
